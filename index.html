What is ICPC and what do I teach?

The International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) is the oldest, largest, and most prestigious algorithmic competitive programming competition in the world. University teams of three compete under extreme time limits to solve complex computational and mathematical problems using optimized algorithms and data structures.

Advanced Data Structures

  • Segment Trees & Lazy Propagation
  • Fenwick Trees (Binary Indexed Tree)
  • Disjoint Set Union (DSU / Union-Find)
  • Trie & Suffix Arrays

Graph Theory & Traversal

  • Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall
  • Minimum Spanning Tree (Kruskal, Prim)
  • Breadth-First & Depth-First Search (BFS/DFS)
  • Topological Sort & Bridges/Cut Vertices

Dynamic Programming & Math

  • 1D & 2D Dynamic Programming
  • DP with Bitmasking & Optimization
  • Greedy Algorithms & Two Pointers
  • Number Theory, Modular Arithmetic, Sieve
